Shorts Factory

A self-hosted studio for producing and publishing your own short videos.

Shorts Factory is a private, self-hosted application that a single creator runs on their own machine. It drafts short-form video ideas and scripts, generates narration and subtitles, assembles the final video, and — after the creator reviews and approves it — publishes that video to the creator's own connected accounts.

How it works

  • Produce. The app writes a script, records narration, generates subtitles and renders a vertical video.
  • Review. Every video lands in an approval queue. Nothing is published without the operator explicitly approving it in the dashboard.
  • Publish. Approved videos are uploaded to the creator's own YouTube channel and/or TikTok account through the platforms' official APIs.

TikTok integration

Shorts Factory connects to TikTok using Login Kit and the Content Posting API. The creator authorizes their own account and grants two permissions:

  • user.info.basic — to show which account is connected.
  • video.publish — to post the creator's own approved videos.

Videos are only posted to the account that granted access, and only when the operator clicks Publish to TikTok. Videos shorter than one minute are rejected before upload. Content produced by the app is declared as AI-generated.

Who it is for

A single creator managing their own channels. The app is not a multi-user service, has no public sign-up, and does not act on behalf of anyone other than the person running it.
