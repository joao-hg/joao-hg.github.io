Privacy Policy

Shorts Factory · Last updated: 24 August 2026

Shorts Factory ("the App") is a self-hosted application operated by a single person on their own computer or server. There is no hosted service, no public sign-up, and no central database operated by the developer. This policy describes what the App stores on the operator's own machine and what it sends to third-party platforms.

1. Who controls the data

The person who installs and runs the App is the sole controller of all data it holds. The developer of the App does not receive, host, or have access to any of it.

2. What the App stores

  • Content produced by the operator — video ideas, scripts, narration audio, subtitles, rendered video files, titles, descriptions and hashtags.
  • Publication records — which video was sent to which platform, when, the resulting platform identifier (such as a TikTok publish_id), and any error returned.
  • TikTok account data — when the operator connects a TikTok account, the App stores the access token, the refresh token, the account's open_id and the granted permission scopes. Basic profile information (display name, username, avatar URL) may be requested to show which account is connected; it is not written to disk.
  • Google/YouTube account data — an OAuth token for the operator's own YouTube channel, when that integration is used.

All of this is stored locally: content and records in a SQLite database file, tokens in a separate file readable only by the account running the App (file permissions 0600). Tokens are never displayed in the interface, never written to logs, and never returned by the App's own API.

3. What the App sends to TikTok

When the operator clicks "Publish to TikTok", the App sends to TikTok's official Content Posting API: the video file itself, the title and hashtags the operator wrote, the chosen privacy level, interaction settings (comment/duet/stitch), and a declaration that the content is AI-generated. Nothing else is transmitted.

4. What the App does not do

  • It does not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.
  • It does not run analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • It does not collect data about TikTok users other than the connected account owner.
  • It does not read, download, or store other people's TikTok content.
  • It does not transmit data to the App's developer.

5. Permissions requested from TikTok

  • user.info.basic — to identify and display the connected account so the operator can confirm they are posting to the right place.
  • video.publish — to post the operator's own approved videos to that account.

These permissions are used for those purposes only. Access applies solely to the account that granted it.

6. Retention and deletion

Data stays on the operator's machine until the operator deletes it. TikTok tokens can be deleted at any time from the App's settings screen ("Disconnect"), which removes the stored token file. Access can also be revoked from the TikTok app under Settings → Security & permissions → Manage app permissions. Deleting the App's data directory removes everything the App holds.

7. Security

Tokens are stored with restricted file permissions and are never exposed through the interface or logs. All communication with TikTok and Google uses HTTPS. Because the App is self-hosted, the security of the underlying machine is the operator's responsibility.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under the minimum age required by the platforms it connects to.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
